# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a friendly, safe, and welcoming community.
We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or hateful conduct
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

- Report incidents to maintainers via a private channel (issue with limited details or email via the repository owner's profile).
- Maintainers may take any appropriate action, including temporary or permanent bans.

If you can, prefer a private report first; please avoid posting sensitive details publicly.
